Scratch Practice Station — V95 hardware backlight overlays v2

These overlays are designed as recessed backlighting, not a filled glow.
The button face remains dark; light leaks from the seams/inner frame and softly lifts the printed label/icon.
Tone: warm copper/amber matched to the existing V95 deck illumination.

Files:
- deck-buttons-backlight-idle.png : subtle always-on backlight for all 5 buttons
- deck-button-prev-backlight-active.png
- deck-button-play-backlight-active.png
- deck-button-stop-backlight-active.png
- deck-button-next-backlight-active.png
- deck-button-auto-backlight-active.png

All overlays are 1536x1024 transparent PNGs and stack directly over cassette-mechanism-pixel-v95.png.
Suggested opacity: idle 0.55–0.75; hover/touch active 0.65–0.85; persistent PLAY/AUTO active 0.85–1.0.
